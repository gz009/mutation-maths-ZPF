\subsection{The whole architecture on one page}

Everything Part VI does is an assembly. Below is the full dependency graph: bodily experience at the
foot, the equation at the crown. Read it upward. Each arrow is a metaphor or a blend that
\emph{exports inferential structure} from the level below to the level above.
