\begin{plain}
We are sequencing the genome about three times more deeply than the biology needs. That is a
reasonable safety margin and I am not suggesting we cut it blindly. But it is worth knowing that the
margin is protecting us from \emph{our own processing choices}, not from the bacterium --- and that
against the one failure mode that actually bit us, the margin does nothing at all.
\end{plain}
