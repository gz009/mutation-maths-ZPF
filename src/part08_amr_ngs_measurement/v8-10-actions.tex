\textbf{3} & Report best-hit \emph{versus best competing family} for every gene call, not just for PVL &

\textbf{4} & Report copy number as an interval, using integer constraints + graph balance &
 A day or two &
 Turns Limitation 3 (``the $\sim$42.4 kb $\varphi$Sa3 span is an estimate'') into a stated confidence

 Attacks the one limit that is genuinely information-theoretic. More short reads cannot.\\

\begin{plain}
None of this says our result is wrong. It is not --- the validation reproduced the published
findings, and §3.4 shows the underlying data is genuinely good. What it says is that we are paying
for safety in the wrong currency, and that the one fault which actually reached our data is the one
kind that paying more cannot fix.
\end{plain}
