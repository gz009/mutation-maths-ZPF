\section{Quantum biology: an honest ledger}\label{sec:qbio}
\markboth{Quantum biology}{}

This field has a credibility problem, and the pasted text presents it without grading. A student
handed that version will be misled --- not about the mathematics, but about which phenomena are
actually there to be modelled. Here is the spread.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.4cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{5.1cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{8.8cm}@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{quantum!13}
\textbf{\color{quantum}Status} & \textbf{\color{quantum}Phenomenon} & \textbf{\color{quantum}Evidence}\\
\midrule
\rowcolor{bio!13}
\textbf{SOLID} & Hydrogen tunnelling in enzyme catalysis &
 Well established and uncontroversial. Kinetic isotope effects far larger than semiclassical theory
 permits. Model with standard rate theory plus tunnelling corrections.\\
\addlinespace[2pt]
\rowcolor{bio!7}
\textbf{STRONG, not closed} & Radical-pair magnetoreception in migratory birds &
 Proposed by Schulten et al.\ 1978; cryptochrome identified as the candidate protein. Hore \&
 Mouritsen 2016 (\textit{Annu.\ Rev.\ Biophys.}) is the standard review; Xu et al., \textit{Nature}
 2021, showed magnetic sensitivity of robin cryptochrome-4 \emph{in vitro}. Behavioural link is
 strong; the \emph{in vivo} chain is not yet closed.\\
\addlinespace[2pt]
\rowcolor{sand!16}
\textbf{SUBSTANTIALLY REVISED} & ``Long-lived electronic coherence'' in photosynthetic light
 harvesting &
 Engel et al., \textit{Nature}, 2007 was the celebrated result. Subsequent work attributes much of
 the observed long-lived beating to \textbf{vibrational}, not electronic, coherence. \textbf{The
 strong original claims did not survive.} Some coherent transport physics remains; the headline does
 not.\\
\addlinespace[2pt]
\rowcolor{crimson!10}
\textbf{WIDELY REJECTED} & Orchestrated objective reduction in microtubules (Penrose--Hameroff) &
 Tegmark, \textit{Phys.\ Rev.\ E}, 2000, estimated decoherence times in the brain many orders of
 magnitude too short. This is the standard objection and it has not been answered.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
