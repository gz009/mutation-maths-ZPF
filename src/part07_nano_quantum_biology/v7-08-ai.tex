\section{AI as the new instrument --- what is actually established}\label{sec:ai}
\markboth{AI as instrument}{}

The pasted text's most forward-looking claim is that AI may discover invariants humans would miss,
and that mathematics will become a human--machine partnership. This is correct, and unlike the rest
of that section it can be supported with specific results rather than expectation.

\begin{critbox}[title={Two cautions worth passing to your students}]
\textbf{1. Prediction is not understanding.} AlphaFold2 predicts structures superbly and explains
folding not at all. It is an instrument, in exactly the sense of Move 4 --- and like every instrument
it generates phenomena that then need a theory. Treating a trained model as if it were a theory is
the modern form of Benjamin Peirce's error.\\[5pt]
\textbf{2. The ``why?'' step has not been automated.} In the \textit{Nature} 2021 work, the machine
supplied a \emph{signal}; humans supplied the concept, the conjecture and the proof. The loop
currently has a human-shaped hole in the middle of it, and Part VI is a book about what fills that
hole.
\end{critbox}
