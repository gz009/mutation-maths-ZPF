\begin{plain}
The lesson is the same one Part VIII made about the sequencing run, turned on its author: a method
can be correctly executed and still produce a confidently wrong answer, because the failure was in
the \emph{model of where errors come from}, not in the arithmetic. I searched for titles containing
physics keywords and concluded absence from silence. Feynman's book is filed under
\emph{Mathematical Statistics}; Axler's measure theory is filed under \emph{Real Analysis}. Neither
name contains the word I was grepping for.
\end{plain}
